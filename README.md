Coupons and cashback are different types of savings.

A typical online purchase may look like this:

2. You add any available Target Circle offer to your account.
3. You open the Target shopping session through ShopBack.
4. You complete the order on Target’s US website.
5. ShopBack records the purchase as tracked or pending.
6. Target validates the transaction.
7. The approved cashback becomes confirmed and can later be withdrawn.

The ShopBack Target page currently lists cashback tracking in approximately **two days** and confirmation in up to **90 days**. The confirmation period is longer because the merchant may need to account for returns, cancellations, exchanges, and other changes to the order.

This means cashback is not an instant coupon code. It will not necessarily reduce the Target checkout total. It is a later reward connected to an eligible purchase.

Suppose your eligible Target order contains $100 of qualifying home products. At a listed cashback rate of 2%, the potential cashback would be $2 before any exclusions or order adjustments.

That amount is based on the eligible purchase subtotal, not necessarily the full amount charged to your card. Taxes, shipping, service fees, delivery fees, and excluded products generally do not count toward cashback.

If you also apply a valid Target Circle coupon, the order may receive both types of savings, but only when the coupon is allowed under ShopBack’s merchant terms. ShopBack specifically warns that purchases made with vouchers or promo codes not featured on its platform may be excluded from cashback.

That is the part worth checking before checkout. A coupon that saves $10 immediately may be better than a small cashback amount, but using an unlisted code could also cause the cashback to be rejected.

## Target coupon rules that affect the final price

Target’s coupon policy includes several rules that matter when combining offers.

### Item-level coupons and category coupons

Target allows certain item-level coupons to be used with category or storewide coupons when the requirements for both offers are met. For example, an item-level coupon may apply to body wash while a separate personal-care coupon applies to a qualifying category threshold.

The threshold is generally calculated using the retail value of qualifying items before certain item-level discounts are applied. That means a coupon may still qualify even if another coupon reduces the final price afterward, but the exact terms depend on the offer.

### Manufacturer coupons

Target accepts some internet-printed and manufacturer coupons when the manufacturer coupon has been added to Target’s database. Target Circle may also display manufacturer coupons that can be used under the stated conditions.

A coupon found on an unrelated coupon website is not automatically accepted. It may be expired, restricted to another retailer, limited to in-store use, or absent from Target’s coupon system.

### Promo codes at Target.com

When a Target coupon is accepted online, Target says it generally includes an alphanumeric code entered at checkout. Promo codes may not apply to taxes, gift cards, or some shipping and handling charges.

Before placing the order, check whether the code applies to:

- The exact product or brand.
- The required minimum spend.
- Online purchases or store purchases.
- Target-sold products only.
- A specific fulfillment method.
- A single use per Target Circle account.
- A specific expiration date.

## Common reasons Target cashback is rejected

Even when an order appears to qualify, cashback can fail for predictable reasons.

### The order contains excluded items

ShopBack may calculate cashback only on eligible products in a mixed cart. In other cases, the entire transaction may be rejected according to the merchant’s terms. The current Target terms exclude broad categories such as groceries, electronics, gift cards, toys, gaming, pharmacy, and pet supplies.

### An unlisted promo code was used

ShopBack warns that purchases using vouchers or promo codes not featured on its platform may not qualify. If the Target coupon provides a large immediate discount but is not listed with the ShopBack offer, you may need to choose between the instant coupon and cashback.

### The purchase was returned or canceled

Returned, exchanged, refunded, rejected, or canceled orders do not qualify for cashback under the current Target terms. Partial returns may also change the cashback amount.

### The order was completed through an excluded fulfillment method

The ShopBack Target terms specify eligibility for orders completed and shipped through the Target US site. Drive Up-only purchases, some pickup workflows, and selected services may be excluded.

### Another referral link overwrote the tracking

Starting from a different cashback site, a coupon extension, or another referral source after activating ShopBack can interfere with attribution. The merchant may credit the last qualifying referral instead.
